About

Sophie Yushina lives in Hamburg. She started out looking at paintings, moved on to making objects, and ended up asking people why they want the things they want.

She is drawn to the quiet questions behind everyday choices: what we buy, what we keep, what we copy, and what it costs the planet.

Now

These days she leads Customer & Market Insights at Rabot Energy. In 2026 she received an iF Design Award.

iF
Writing

She writes about consumer behaviour, technology adoption and conscious consumption. Her next essay, The (Digital) Dupes World, asks what happens when software can be copied as easily as furniture.
